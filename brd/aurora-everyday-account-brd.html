1. Purpose

Customers can currently only open an Everyday Account in branch or by phone. Abandonment in the phone channel is 41%. This document specifies the business requirements for a self-service digital origination journey on mobile, to be delivered as a click-through prototype for stakeholder sign-off before build.

2. In scope

  • Mobile web, single applicant
  • Everyday Account and its variants
  • Capture, review and confirmation of an application

3. Out of scope

  • Identity verification and credit decisioning
  • Core banking integration — no data is submitted
  • Existing-customer authentication

4. Business requirements

RefRequirementPriority
REQ-001 The journey shall open with a product introduction screen stating the account name and its three headline benefits: no monthly account fee, opening in under five minutes, and an instant digital card. Must
REQ-002 The customer shall be able to start an application from the introduction screen in a single action. Must
REQ-003 The application shall capture the applicant’s full name, email address and date of birth, and shall allow selection of an account type from: Everyday Account; Everyday Account + Savings; Student Everyday Account. Must
REQ-004 The customer shall be prevented from progressing while any mandatory field is missing or invalid, and shall be shown which field is at fault and why. Must
REQ-005 Before submission the customer shall be shown a review screen displaying every value they entered, for confirmation. Must
REQ-006 The customer shall explicitly accept the Product Disclosure Statement and Target Market Determination before the application can be submitted. Must
REQ-007 On submission the customer shall be shown a confirmation screen containing their new BSB and account number, and an option to add the digital card to their wallet. Must
REQ-008 The customer shall be able to see how far through the application they are, and to return to a previous step without losing entered data. Should

5. Business rules

RefRule
BR-01Full name is mandatory and must be at least two characters.
BR-02Email address is mandatory and must be a structurally valid address.
BR-03Date of birth is mandatory and must be entered as DD / MM / YYYY.
BR-04The application cannot be submitted until the disclosure acceptance in REQ-006 is given.
BR-05No application data is transmitted or stored. This is a prototype for sign-off only.

6. Brand and visual direction

The journey shall use the existing Aurora mobile design system without modification — the same colour styles, type scale and spacing already in use across the Aurora app. Target frame is 390 × 844.

7. Acceptance criteria

  • A stakeholder can complete the journey end to end on a phone-sized screen without assistance.
  • Every screen traces to at least one requirement reference above.
  • Invalid input is refused with a visible, specific message (REQ-004, BR-01 to BR-03).
  • The review screen shows the data the tester actually entered, not sample data (REQ-005).

Note for delivery: this is a sign-off prototype, not a production build. No backend, no data capture, no integration. It exists so the business can agree the journey before development is scheduled.
