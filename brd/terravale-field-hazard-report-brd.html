1. Purpose

Hazards and safety observations are currently raised on paper triplicate forms collected at the end of shift. In the last quarter, 38% of forms were incomplete or illegible, and the median time from observation to supervisor awareness was 9.4 hours. This document specifies the business requirements for a mobile hazard reporting journey for site personnel, to be delivered as a click-through prototype for stakeholder sign-off before build.

2. In scope

  • Mobile handset, single reporter
  • Hazards and safety observations
  • Capture, review and confirmation of one report

3. Out of scope

  • Injury and environmental incident reporting
  • Integration with the HSE case management system
  • Corrective action tracking and close-out

4. Business requirements

RefRequirementPriority
REQ-001 The journey shall open with an introduction screen naming the tool and stating its three headline benefits: reports can be raised without mobile signal, no sign-in is required on site, and the report reaches the shift supervisor immediately. Must
REQ-002 The reporter shall be able to start a hazard report from the introduction screen in a single action. Must
REQ-003 The report shall capture the reporter’s name, their contact email, the date the hazard was observed, and a hazard category selected from: Slip, trip or fall; Vehicle and mobile plant; Working at height; Electrical; Environmental spill. Must
REQ-004 The reporter shall be prevented from progressing while any mandatory field is missing or invalid, and shall be shown which field is at fault and why. Must
REQ-005 Before submission the reporter shall be shown a review screen displaying every value they entered, for confirmation. Must
REQ-006 The reporter shall explicitly confirm the declaration — that the details are accurate and that they have made the area safe where it was safe to do so — before the report can be submitted. Must
REQ-007 On submission the reporter shall be shown a confirmation screen containing the hazard reference number allocated to their report, and an option to notify their shift supervisor. Must
REQ-008 The reporter shall be able to see how far through the report they are, and to return to a previous step without losing entered data. Should

5. Business rules

RefRule
BR-01Reporter name is mandatory and must be at least two characters.
BR-02Contact email is mandatory and must be a structurally valid address.
BR-03Date observed is mandatory and must be entered as DD / MM / YYYY.
BR-04The report cannot be submitted until the declaration in REQ-006 is confirmed.
BR-05No report data is transmitted or stored. This is a prototype for sign-off only.

6. Brand and visual direction

The journey shall use the existing Terravale mobile design system without modification — the same colour styles, type scale and spacing already in use across Terravale field applications. Target frame is 390 × 844. Controls must remain usable in gloves and in direct sunlight.

7. Acceptance criteria

  • A stakeholder can complete a hazard report end to end on a phone-sized screen without assistance.
  • Every screen traces to at least one requirement reference above.
  • Invalid input is refused with a visible, specific message (REQ-004, BR-01 to BR-03).
  • The review screen shows the data the tester actually entered, not sample data (REQ-005).

Note for delivery: this is a sign-off prototype, not a production build. No backend, no data capture, no integration with the HSE case management system. It exists so the business can agree the journey before development is scheduled.
